O Surface · About

I am a PhD candidate in Interdisciplinary Ecology (Soil and Water Science) in the University of Florida's School of Natural Resources and Environment, and a Water Scholars Graduate Fellow in the UF Water Institute's BREWS program. I am based at the UF/IFAS Gulf Coast Research and Education Center (GCREC) in Wimauma, Florida, working with Dr. Mary G. Lusk.

My research covers integrated soil management to increase soil carbon sequestration, reduce nutrient losses and boost crop productivity. I work with biochar, composted biosolids, organic manure and cover crops, and on the beneficial reuse of reclaimed water for irrigation. Before Florida, I trained in soil chemistry and fertility at Obafemi Awolowo University in Nigeria and the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul in Brazil.

A Topsoil · Research

Bridging environmental sustainability and agricultural productivity

Sustainable soil management

Practices that improve soil health, raise soil carbon sequestration and manage nutrient cycling to cut losses and improve crop nutrient use.

Integrated practices

How biochar, biosolids, organic manure and cover cropping, alone and together, change soil quality and crop performance.

Water resource management

Beneficial reuse of reclaimed water for agricultural irrigation, and limiting nutrient leaching from sandy soils.

Dissertation

Integrated Impacts of Soil Amendments and Cover Crops on Sandy Soil Health, Carbon, and Nutrients

  • Split-plot randomized complete block field trial at GCREC: 36 plots, with bell pepper as the test crop.
  • Treatments combine biochar and composted biosolids with sunn hemp and sorghum-sudangrass cover crops.
  • Measures greenhouse gas fluxes (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O) with a LI-COR Smart Chamber, nitrate, ammonium and phosphate leaching at 50 cm with lysimeters, soil enzyme activity and crop yield.
